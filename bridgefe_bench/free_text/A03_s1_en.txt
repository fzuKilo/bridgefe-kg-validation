Quick note from site. It's a single simply supported span, spans 25 m bearing to bearing, deck 16 m wide, 5 small box girders per span, C40 throughout. Section is the usual one: middle-girder section at midspan: depth 1.4 m; top width 2.40 m; flange 0.18 m thick at the tip, thickening by 6.2 cm over a 20.1 cm long haunch next to the web; bottom slab 1.00 m wide and 0.18 m thick; top slab 0.18 m thick; webs 0.20 m thick (measured horizontally) with outer faces inclined at 1:3.5; 15 cm x 7 cm chamfers at the upper corners of the cell. Bearings: plain elastomeric bearings at Abutment 0 and Abutment 1 (2/girder end, 30 cm by 35 cm, 4 rubber layers of 0.8 cm).  Traffic is one-way on a 15 m carriageway, 4 lanes, designed for Highway Class II. Girder self-weight only, nothing else on it yet.
