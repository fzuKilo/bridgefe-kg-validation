Quick note from site. It's a precast unit made continuous after erection, spans 20 + 25 + 25 + 20 m bearing to bearing, deck 16 m wide, 6 T girders per span, C50 throughout. Section is the usual one: middle-girder section at midspan: depth 1.7 m; precast flange 1.80 m wide, 0.18 m thick over the outer 0.20 m and thickening linearly to 0.27 m at the web; web 0.20 m thick; bottom bulb 0.60 m wide with a 0.20 m vertical part and a 0.20 m high linear transition to the web. Bearings: sliding (PTFE) bearings at Abutment 0, Pier 2, Pier 3 and Abutment 4 (1/girder end, 30 cm by 35 cm, 4 rubber layers of 0.8 cm); fixed (plain elastomeric) bearings at Pier 1 (1/girder end, 30 cm by 35 cm, 4 rubber layers of 0.8 cm).  Traffic is one-way on a 15 m carriageway, 4 lanes, designed for Highway Class I. Girder self-weight only, nothing else on it yet.
