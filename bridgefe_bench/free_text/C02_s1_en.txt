Quick note from site. It's a cast-in-place continuous box girder, spans 35 + 45 + 35 m bearing to bearing, deck 12.5 m wide, one single-cell box, C50 throughout. Section is the usual one: single-cell box section of constant depth 2.2 m: top slab 12.5 m wide and 0.25 m thick; bottom slab 6.5 m wide and 0.22 m thick; two vertical webs 0.45 m thick; cantilever flanges 0.5 m thick at the root tapering linearly to 0.2 m at the tip. Also use Ec = 37000 MPa from the material tests. Bearings: sliding (PTFE) bearings at Abutment 0 and Abutment 3 (2/line, 40 cm by 50 cm, 5 rubber layers of 1.1 cm); fixed (plain elastomeric) bearings at Pier 1 (2/line, 60 cm by 70 cm, 7 rubber layers of 1.1 cm); sliding (PTFE) bearings at Pier 2 (2/line, 55 cm by 65 cm, 6 rubber layers of 1.1 cm). Piers are twin 1.8 m round columns (C40), fixed at the footing, heights 20, 27 m from Pier 1 onward. Traffic is one-way on a 11.5 m carriageway, 3 lanes, designed for Highway Class I. Girder self-weight only, nothing else on it yet.
