[Span arrangement] One cast-in-place continuous unit of three spans, 36 + 48 + 36 m (centre-to-centre of bearings); deck width 16 m.
[Section and material] Cast-in-place prestressed concrete box girder, single-cell box section of constant depth 2.4 m: top slab 16 m wide and 0.25 m thick; bottom slab 9 m wide and 0.22 m thick; two vertical webs 0.45 m thick; cantilever flanges 0.5 m thick at the root tapering linearly to 0.2 m at the tip; girder concrete C50. Consider the self-weight of the main girders only (no wet joints, diaphragms, pavement or barriers).
[Bearings and boundary] Abutment 0 and Abutment 3: PTFE sliding laminated elastomeric bearings, two per support line; effective plan size 400 mm x 500 mm, five internal rubber layers 11 mm thick; shear modulus of the rubber 1.2 MPa (manufacturer's test value). Pier 1: plain laminated elastomeric bearings (fixed), two per support line; effective plan size 600 mm x 700 mm, seven internal rubber layers 11 mm thick; shear modulus of the rubber 1.2 MPa (manufacturer's test value). Pier 2: PTFE sliding laminated elastomeric bearings, two per support line; effective plan size 550 mm x 650 mm, six internal rubber layers 11 mm thick; shear modulus of the rubber 1.2 MPa (manufacturer's test value). Piers: twin circular columns of 1.6 m diameter, concrete C40, fixed at the base; height from base to bearings: Pier 1 8 m; Pier 2 11 m.
[Design standard] Highway Class I loading (JTG D60-2015); one-way traffic, carriageway width 15 m, four design lanes.
