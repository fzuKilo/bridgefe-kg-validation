[Span arrangement] Single simply supported span of 20 m (centre-to-centre of bearings); deck width 9 m.
[Section and material] Precast prestressed T girders, 4 per span; middle-girder section at midspan: depth 1.35 m; precast flange 1.80 m wide, 0.18 m thick over the outer 0.20 m and thickening linearly to 0.27 m at the web; web 0.20 m thick; bottom bulb 0.60 m wide with a 0.20 m vertical part and a 0.20 m high linear transition to the web; girder concrete C40. Consider the self-weight of the main girders only (no wet joints, diaphragms, pavement or barriers).
[Bearings and boundary] Abutments at both ends. Abutment 0 and Abutment 1: plain laminated elastomeric bearings, one under each girder end; effective plan size 250 mm x 300 mm, four internal rubber layers 8 mm thick.
[Design standard] Highway Class I loading (JTG D60-2015); one-way traffic, carriageway width 8 m, two design lanes.
