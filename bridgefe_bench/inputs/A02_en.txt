[Span arrangement] Single simply supported span of 25 m (centre-to-centre of bearings); deck width 10 m.
[Section and material] Precast prestressed small box girders, 3 per span; middle-girder section at midspan: depth 1.4 m; top width 2.40 m; flange 0.18 m thick at the tip, thickening by 6.2 cm over a 20.1 cm long haunch next to the web; bottom slab 1.00 m wide and 0.18 m thick; top slab 0.18 m thick; webs 0.20 m thick (measured horizontally) with outer faces inclined at 1:3.5; 15 cm x 7 cm chamfers at the upper corners of the cell; girder concrete C40. Consider the self-weight of the main girders only (no wet joints, diaphragms, pavement or barriers).
[Bearings and boundary] Abutments at both ends. Abutment 0 and Abutment 1: plain laminated elastomeric bearings, two under each girder end; effective plan size 300 mm x 350 mm, four internal rubber layers 8 mm thick.
[Design standard] Highway Class I loading (JTG D60-2015); two-way traffic, carriageway width 9 m, two design lanes.
