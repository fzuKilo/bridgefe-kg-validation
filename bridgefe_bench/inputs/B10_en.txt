[Span arrangement] One continuous unit of five spans, 20 + 25 + 25 + 25 + 20 m (centre-to-centre of bearings), precast girders simply supported during erection and made continuous afterwards; deck width 10 m.
[Section and material] Precast prestressed T girders, 4 per span; middle-girder section at midspan: depth 1.7 m; precast flange 1.80 m wide, 0.18 m thick over the outer 0.20 m and thickening linearly to 0.27 m at the web; web 0.20 m thick; bottom bulb 0.60 m wide with a 0.20 m vertical part and a 0.20 m high linear transition to the web; girder concrete C50. Consider the self-weight of the main girders only (no wet joints, diaphragms, pavement or barriers).
[Bearings and boundary] Abutment 0, Pier 1, Pier 2, Pier 4 and Abutment 5: PTFE sliding laminated elastomeric bearings, one under each girder end; effective plan size 300 mm x 350 mm, four internal rubber layers 8 mm thick. Pier 3: plain laminated elastomeric bearings (fixed), one under each girder end; effective plan size 300 mm x 350 mm, four internal rubber layers 8 mm thick.
[Design standard] Highway Class I loading (JTG D60-2015); two-way traffic, carriageway width 9 m, two design lanes.
