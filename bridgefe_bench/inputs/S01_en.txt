[Span arrangement] One continuous unit of six spans, 30 + 30 + 30 + 30 + 30 + 30 m (centre-to-centre of bearings), precast girders simply supported during erection and made continuous afterwards; deck width 12.5 m.
[Section and material] Precast prestressed small box girders, 4 per span; middle-girder section at midspan: depth 1.6 m; top width 2.40 m; flange 0.18 m thick at the tip, thickening by 6.3 cm over a 19.2 cm long haunch next to the web; bottom slab 1.00 m wide and 0.18 m thick; top slab 0.18 m thick; webs 0.20 m thick (measured horizontally) with outer faces inclined at 1:4; 15 cm x 7 cm chamfers at the upper corners of the cell; girder concrete C50. Consider the self-weight of the main girders only (no wet joints, diaphragms, pavement or barriers).
[Bearings and boundary] Abutment 0, Pier 1, Pier 2, Pier 4, Pier 5 and Abutment 6: PTFE sliding laminated elastomeric bearings, two under each girder end; effective plan size 300 mm x 400 mm, five internal rubber layers 8 mm thick. Pier 3: plain laminated elastomeric bearings (fixed), two under each girder end; effective plan size 300 mm x 400 mm, five internal rubber layers 8 mm thick.
[Design standard] Highway Class I loading (JTG D60-2015); one-way traffic, carriageway width 11.5 m, three design lanes.
