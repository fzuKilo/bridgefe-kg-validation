[Span arrangement] Single simply supported span of 40 m (centre-to-centre of bearings); deck width 10 m.
[Section and material] Precast prestressed T girders, 4 per span; middle-girder section at midspan: depth 2.5 m; precast flange 1.80 m wide, 0.18 m thick over the outer 0.20 m and thickening linearly to 0.27 m at the web; web 0.20 m thick; bottom bulb 0.60 m wide with a 0.35 m vertical part and a 0.20 m high linear transition to the web; girder concrete C50. Consider the self-weight of the main girders only (no wet joints, diaphragms, pavement or barriers).
[Bearings and boundary] Abutments at both ends. Abutment 0: spherical bearings GQZ2000 (JT/T 854-2013, vertical design capacity 2000 kN), one under each girder end, longitudinally movable (types SX/DX). Abutment 1: spherical bearings GQZ2000 (JT/T 854-2013, vertical design capacity 2000 kN), one under each girder end, longitudinally fixed (fixed type GD at middle girders, transversely movable type DX at edge girders).
[Design standard] Highway Class I loading (JTG D60-2015); two-way traffic, carriageway width 9 m, two design lanes.
