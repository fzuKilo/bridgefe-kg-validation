[Span arrangement] One continuous unit of four spans, 25 + 25 + 25 + 25 m (centre-to-centre of bearings), precast girders simply supported during erection and made continuous afterwards; deck width 10 m.
[Section and material] Steel plate girders acting compositely with a 0.25 m cast-in-place deck slab, 3 per span; steel girder depth 1.4 m, top flange 400 mm x 25 mm, bottom flange 500 mm x 30 mm, web 1345 mm x 16 mm, steel Q345; deck concrete C50. Consider the self-weight of the main girders only (no wet joints, diaphragms, pavement or barriers).
[Bearings and boundary] Abutment 0, Pier 1, Pier 3 and Abutment 4: PTFE sliding laminated elastomeric bearings, two under each girder end; effective plan size 300 mm x 350 mm, four internal rubber layers 8 mm thick. Pier 2: plain laminated elastomeric bearings (fixed), two under each girder end; effective plan size 300 mm x 350 mm, four internal rubber layers 8 mm thick.
[Design standard] Highway Class II loading (JTG D60-2015); two-way traffic, carriageway width 9 m, two design lanes.
